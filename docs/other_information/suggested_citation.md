## Suggested Citation
We hope that you find ECHO-AIR useful in your scientific endeavors. If you do use our code, below is our recommended citation. 

ECHO-AIR. Version X.XX. University of California, Berkeley and California Office of Environmental Health Hazard Assessment; 2025. Accessed Month DD, 2025. [https://echo-air-model.github.io/](https://echo-air-model.github.io/) 

Be sure to replace the version number with the number that is printed at the top of your control file and the date you most recently accessed it.

You may also consider citing Koolik et al. (2024), which is the first peer-reviewed publication to use the ECHO-AIR model. That suggested citation is below.

Libby H. Koolik et al., PM<sub>2.5</sub> exposure disparities persist despite strict vehicle emissions controls in California. <it>Sci. Adv.</it> <b>10</b>, eadn8544(2024). [DOI:10.1126/sciadv.adn8544](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn8544)
